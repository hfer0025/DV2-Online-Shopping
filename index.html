Add to Cart: How the World Shops Online

From global habits to Australian wallets: who buys online, when, what they buy, and where it comes from.

01

The Global Cart

Online shopping is near-universal in some countries and rare in others. In 2021, 77% of Australian adults bought something online, close to the Nordic leaders, while in much of South Asia and Africa fewer than 1 in 10 did.

02

Australia Logs On

A decade of steady growth, then COVID lockdowns pushed online sales into a new normal. Annual online retail sales rose from $20.8B in 2019 to $49.9B in 2024, and every year November and December break records.

03

Where in Australia

The big eastern states spend the most in total: NSW alone spent $26.4B. But divide by population and the picture changes: Canberrans are the most active online shoppers, and the Northern Territory trails well behind.

04

What We Buy and Who Buys It

Marketplaces like Amazon, eBay and Temu take the biggest slice. Department stores grew fastest of the big categories, and Millennials spend more than a third of every online dollar.

05

Beyond Our Borders

43% of Australian online shoppers buy from overseas, mostly from the USA and China. Lower prices pull them in; slow delivery and wanting to support local businesses hold them back.
